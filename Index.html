# Create a Bamboo Bottle Online Store Website Create a modern, attractive, eco-friendly, and fully responsive **Bamboo Bottle Online Shopping Website** using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. ## Website Name **BambooBliss – Eco-Friendly Bottles & Accessories** ### 1. Header * Create a beautiful logo with a bamboo leaf and bottle icon. * Website name: BambooBliss. * Navigation menu: Home, Shop, Bamboo Bottles, Accessories, About Us, Contact. * Add a search bar to search products. * Add Login/Register buttons. * Add a shopping cart icon with a live cart counter. * Make the header sticky while scrolling. * Use a natural green, beige, and white color theme. ### 2. Hero Image Slider Create an automatic image slider with attractive, high-quality bamboo bottle photos. Include these slides: * Slide 1: Premium Bamboo Water Bottles – “Stay Hydrated, Stay Natural.” * Slide 2: Eco-Friendly Bottles – “Choose a Greener Future.” * Slide 3: Bamboo Bottle Accessories – “Everything You Need in One Place.” Features: * Automatic sliding. * Previous and next buttons. * Slider indicators. * Responsive design for mobile and desktop. * Add a Shop Now button on every slide. ### 3. Product Categories Display beautiful product cards with photos for these categories: * Bamboo Water Bottles * Stainless Steel Bamboo Bottles * Glass Bottles with Bamboo Covers * Insulated Bamboo Bottles * Kids' Bamboo Bottles * Bamboo Travel Bottles * Personalized Bamboo Bottles * Bamboo Bottle Gift Sets ### 4. Accessories Section Include a complete collection of bamboo bottle accessories: * Replacement bamboo lids * Stainless steel caps * Leak-proof silicone seals * Replacement straws * Bottle cleaning brushes * Bottle carrying bags * Protective bottle sleeves * Bottle holders * Bottle straps * Bottle covers * Insulation pouches * Gift boxes * Spare gaskets * Bottle stands * Cleaning kits Every accessory should have a product image, name, price, short description, and Add to Cart button. ### 5. Product Cards Each product card must include: * High-quality product image. * Product name. * Price in Indian rupees (₹). * Original price and discount, where applicable. * Star rating. * Short product description. * Product size and capacity. * Available colors and designs. * Add to Cart button. * Buy Now button. * Wishlist heart icon. Use sample product prices and clearly label them as demo prices. ### 6. Special Offers Section Create an attractive promotional section featuring: * Buy 2 bottles and get a special discount. * Eco-friendly bottle combo packs. * Bottle and accessory gift sets. * Seasonal offers. * Best-selling products. * New arrivals. Include Shop Now buttons and promotional banners. ### 7. Shopping Cart Functionality Use JavaScript to implement: * Add products to cart. * Remove products from cart. * Increase or decrease product quantities. * Automatically calculate subtotal and total. * Update the cart counter. * Show an empty-cart message. * Save cart contents in localStorage. * Display a checkout button. Create a demo checkout page or checkout modal. Do not collect real payment details. ### 8. About Us Section Add an attractive section describing BambooBliss as an eco-conscious store offering reusable bamboo-style bottles and practical accessories. Highlight: * Reusable product choices. * Plastic-reduction awareness. * Stylish designs. * Convenient everyday hydration. * Thoughtful gift options. Avoid unsupported claims about product materials, sustainability certifications, or health benefits. ### 9. Customer Reviews Display sample customer review cards with: * Customer name. * Star rating. * Short review. * Product purchased. Clearly treat reviews as demonstration content. ### 10. Newsletter Section Add a newsletter subscription form with: * Email input. * Subscribe button. * JavaScript validation. * A success message after submission. ### 11. Footer Create a complete footer with four columns: **Company** * About Us * Our Story * Contact Us **Shop** * All Bottles * Bottle Accessories * Best Sellers * Special Offers **Customer Support** * FAQs * Shipping Information * Returns and Refunds * Order Tracking **Follow Us** * Instagram * Facebook * YouTube * Pinterest Also include: * Email address and phone placeholders. * Privacy Policy. * Terms and Conditions. * Copyright notice. * Payment method icons for display only. ### 12. Design and Colors Use: * Forest green: #245C3A * Bamboo beige: #E8D8B5 * Cream white: #FAF8F0 * Leaf green: #75A85A * Dark text: #26352B Design style: * Modern and minimal. * Nature-inspired layout. * Rounded product cards. * Soft shadows. * Smooth hover animations. * Attractive typography. * High-quality product photos. * Mobile-friendly navigation. ### 13. Technical Requirements Use separate files: 1. `index.html` – Website structure. 2. `style.css` – Styling and responsive design. 3. `script.js` – Slider, cart, search, filters, wishlist, and form validation. Requirements: * Use semantic HTML5. * Use modern CSS Flexbox and Grid. * Use vanilla JavaScript without requiring a framework. * Add real product images from reliable image URLs. * Ensure all buttons have working demo interactions. * Implement product search and category filters. * Make the slider work automatically and manually. * Add a mobile hamburger menu. * Use localStorage for cart persistence. * Add accessible labels and image alt text. * Include comments in the code. * Ensure there are no JavaScript errors. * Make the website easy to deploy on Vercel. **Final result:** Build a complete, visually appealing, responsive BambooBliss online store with a header, image slider, product catalog, accessories, shopping cart, offers, customer reviews, newsletter, and footer using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
